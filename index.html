Questions

Do my photos leave my phone?
No. Roamory reads where and when your photos were taken on the device. Photos, places and coordinates are never uploaded.

Why does Roamory ask for photo access?
Wrapped, your Atlas and your stamps are built from your photos' locations and dates. Without access the app has nothing to show. Change it in Settings → Roamory → Photos.

What does Roamory Pro add?
Every Wrapped chapter for every year, animated cards exported as 7-second videos with music, no watermark on your year poster, and every day route. The Atlas, stamps, quests and Parties are free. Wrapped 2026 is a one-time purchase for the 2026 story only.

How do I restore a purchase?
Settings → Roamory Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Roamory, or Settings → Roamory Pro → Manage subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete account and data. It removes your account and everything on our server, and clears the app on this phone.

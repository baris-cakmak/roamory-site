Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026. DRAFT – to be reviewed before App Store submission.

Roamory ("we", "the app") helps you see and share your travel year. This policy explains what data we process.

Data we collect

  • Account: an anonymous account is created when you first open the app. If you sign in with Apple, we store your Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email.
  • Travel data you enter: places you visit and trip dates you add in the app. This data is stored to build your Wrapped and Atlas.
  • Party features: if you create or join a party, we store the party membership and the shared result you choose to publish.
  • Diagnostics: crash and error reports via Sentry, which may include device model, OS version and app version.
  • Purchases: subscription and purchase status is handled by Apple and processed through RevenueCat. We do not see your payment details.
  • Advertising: the free version may show ads served by Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers subject to your iOS tracking settings.

How we use data

To provide the app's features, keep your data in sync between your devices, support party features, fix bugs, and manage purchases.

Service providers

Supabase (backend and storage), Sentry (diagnostics), RevenueCat (purchases), Google AdMob (ads), Apple (sign-in and payments).

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app. Deleting your account removes your personal data from our servers, except data we must keep for legal reasons.

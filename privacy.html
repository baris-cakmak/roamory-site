Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026.

Roamory ("we", "the app") helps you see and share your travel year. This policy explains what data we process and where it goes.

What stays on your phone

Your photos are read on your device only to find where and when each one was taken, and to suggest places and stories. Photos are never uploaded to our servers. Walking, running and cycling routes you choose to import from Health are read on your device only and are never sent off the device. Location is used on your device to name the places you visit.

What we collect and store

  • Anonymous account: when you first open the app we create an anonymous account identifier so your data can be synced between your devices.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional): if you choose to sign in, we store your Apple user identifier and, only if you share them, your name and email address.
  • Travel data you create: places, trips, stamps and progress that you add or that are generated from your synced data, so your Wrapped and Atlas work across devices.
  • Party data: if you create or join a Party, we store the membership and the results you choose to share with it.
  • Diagnostics: crash and error reports through Sentry, which may include device model, operating system version and app version.
  • Purchases: subscription and purchase status is handled by Apple and processed through RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.

Advertising and tracking

Roamory does not show ads, does not include any advertising SDK, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Service providers

Supabase (backend, database and storage), Sentry (crash reports), RevenueCat (purchases), Apple (sign-in and payments). Each receives only the data needed to do its job.

How we use data

To provide the app's features, keep your data in sync, support Party features, fix bugs and manage purchases. We do not sell your data.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account and all of your server-side data at any time from Settings → Delete my data. Deletion removes your synced data from our servers; on-device data is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Roamory is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. Contact us using the email address below.

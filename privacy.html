Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026.

Roamory ("we", "the app") helps you see and share your travel year. This policy explains what data we process and where it goes.

What stays on your phone

Your photos are read on your device only to find where and when each one was taken, and to suggest places and stories. Photos are never uploaded to our servers. Walking, running and cycling routes you choose to import from Health are read on your device only and are never sent off the device. Location is used on your device to name the places you visit. Place names, stamps, trips, day routes and your Atlas are built and kept on your device; they are not uploaded. To turn coordinates into place names, the app uses Apple's on-device and Apple Maps services (Apple's privacy policy applies to that lookup).

What we collect and store

  • Anonymous account: when you first open the app we create an anonymous account identifier. It ties your Party memberships and purchases to you without asking for an email.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional): if you choose to sign in, we store your Apple user identifier and, if you share it, your first name. We do not ask for your email address.
  • Profile and yearly totals: the display name and colour you pick (shown to people in your Parties; offensive names are replaced with "Roamer"), and a few yearly totals: your level, number of stamps, countries and cities, cat photos, the hour of your latest photo, your travel persona and favourite minute. No place names, coordinates or photos.
  • Party data: if you create or join a Party, we store the membership so members can see each other's name and totals. You can leave a Party at any time from its results screen, and report a name to us from the same screen.
  • Push notifications: if you allow them, your device's notification token, used only to tell you when someone joins your Party.
  • App usage: a short list of in-app events (for example "onboarding finished", "paywall shown", "share started") stored with your anonymous account identifier, so we can see which features help people and improve them. These events never contain photos, locations or places, and they are not used for advertising.
  • Diagnostics: crash and error reports and a sample of performance measurements through Sentry, which may include device model, operating system version and app version. They are not linked to your account.
  • Purchases: subscription and purchase status is handled by Apple and processed through RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.

Advertising and tracking

Roamory does not show ads, does not include any advertising SDK, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Service providers

Supabase (backend, database and storage), Sentry (crash reports), RevenueCat (purchases), Apple (sign-in and payments). Each receives only the data needed to do its job.

How we use data

To provide the app's features, run Parties, understand which features are used, fix bugs and manage purchases. We do not sell your data.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account and all of your server-side data at any time from Settings → Delete account and data. Deletion removes your account, profile, totals, Party memberships, notification tokens and usage events from our servers, revokes Sign in with Apple, and clears the app's data on this device. Parties expire after 45 days. An anonymous account that has not been used for 180 days and belongs to no Party is deleted automatically. A subscription is billed by Apple: cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Children

Roamory is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
